# Manuscript section evidence packet

The paper is a short descriptive replication note. The introduction and related-evidence sections state that the curve is known and cite BLS, NBER and IMF work. The data section is grounded in `data-provenance.json`, the frozen API responses, and `data/schema.json`. The design is finite-sample conditional arithmetic and does not identify a cause. Results and the 2024 robustness check are generated by `scripts/analyze.py` into `research/results.json`, `output/tables/means.tex`, and `output/figures/beveridge.pdf`. The limitations section prohibits causal or novelty claims. The appendix points to `research/replication.json` for clean replay.

This packet checks presence and traceability of sections. It does not certify source completeness or independent scientific review; those gates remain separate.
