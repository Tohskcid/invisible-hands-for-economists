## Numerical and logical audit

I recalculated directly from the three raw BLS JSON bodies, separately from `scripts/analyze.py`. In the inclusive 3.5-4.5 unemployment interval, 2001-19 has **48** months and mean openings **4.095833%**; 2022-24 has **35** months and mean **5.700000%**; the difference is **1.604167 percentage points**. For 2024 alone, **12** months average **4.650000%**, a **0.554167-point** difference from the same baseline. The secondary 4.0-5.0 band has **75** versus **8** months and means **3.574667%** versus **4.787500%**, a **1.212833-point** difference. `research/results.json`, the displayed table, and the PDF round these values correctly. The raw-to-panel logic in `scripts/analyze.py` pairs months correctly and excludes the 2020-21 transition as described.

The phrase "comparable unemployment" is only a band comparison. Within the primary band, mean unemployment is **4.069%** in the baseline and **3.777%** in 2022-24; lower unemployment in the latter can itself accompany higher vacancies along a negatively sloped curve. As an exploratory check using only the frozen one-decimal unemployment values, reweighting baseline vacancy means to the *post-period's exact unemployment-rate frequencies* reduces the pooled gap to **1.223 points** and the 2024-only gap to **0.296 points**. This check is not a causal adjustment and has thin cells (only one baseline month at 3.5%). It shows why the headline gap should not be read as a measured vertical structural shift. The 2022, 2023, and 2024 conditional vacancy means are **6.850% (12 months), 5.591% (11), and 4.650% (12)**; the narrowing description is arithmetically fair for these selected years. The 2024 value remains above the full baseline mean, so "normalization" is partial and baseline-dependent.

The manuscript explicitly declines causal inference and independent-month standard errors, which is appropriate. Its ex-post years and bands, serial dependence, differing CPS/JOLTS populations, changing composition and recruiting intensity, survey error, and revisions limit external and structural interpretation. The alternative band is weak because only eight comparison months enter. No formal uncertainty analysis, dynamic model, counterfactual, or mechanism test converts the conditional gap into a top-journal empirical contribution. The comparison between the IMF vacancy-rate magnitude and the pilot rate needs the denominator caveat noted above.
