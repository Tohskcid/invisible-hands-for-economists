# Frozen design (delegated PI decision)

Unit is a source row/restaurant; sheet has one duplicate and is not used as a unique key. Preserve source rows with row_id. Treatment is NJ residence and the April 1, 1992 wage floor change. Comparison is surveyed Pennsylvania restaurants.
Primary outcome: full-time-equivalent headcount = full-time nonmanagers + managers + 0.5 times part-time nonmanagers; all components required. Primary sample: outcomes observed in both waves; permanently closed restaurants have zero recorded post employment and remain included. Temporary closures/missing components remain missing. No survey weights.
Primary estimand: difference between mean within-restaurant changes in NJ and PA on complete pairs. It identifies an ATT only under parallel untreated changes, no anticipation relevant to baseline, no spillovers and selection that does not distort this target. One baseline wave cannot test trends; statewide treatment has only two jurisdictions. Report a descriptive change contrast, with conditional causal interpretation stated separately.
Estimator: OLS change on intercept and state; analytical homoskedastic, HC1 and separate-group variance; 2000 stratified restaurant bootstraps seed 19920401 as conditional uncertainty. These are not state-randomization inference.
Supporting checks frozen before estimation: chain and ownership controls; continuous initial-wage gap; alternative part-time weights and exclusion of managers; omit permanent closures; phone-only; omit shore; leave each chain out; winsorize changes at pooled 1/99 percentiles; high-wage NJ versus PA diagnostic; starting wages, opening hours and meal price changes; full-time share. No preferred significance criterion.
The raw/codebook audit establishes observed support and missingness. No event-study pretrends, payroll validation, revenue/profit/welfare or long-run effects are estimable.
