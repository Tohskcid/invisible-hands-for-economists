# Identification at a regression-discontinuity cutoff

## Problem

- identify a local treatment effect at an assignment cutoff
- distinguish sharp and fuzzy regression discontinuity

## Assumptions

- continuity of the relevant potential-outcome regression functions at the cutoff
- a treatment-probability discontinuity for fuzzy RDD
- no precise manipulation that invalidates the local comparison

## Conclusion

Discontinuities in conditional outcomes at the cutoff identify a local treatment effect, with the fuzzy design using the treatment-probability jump as the denominator.

## Method

continuity-based local identification

## Failure conditions

- other determinants changing discontinuously at the cutoff undermine attribution
- weak or absent treatment discontinuity makes fuzzy identification unstable
- the estimand is local to the cutoff
