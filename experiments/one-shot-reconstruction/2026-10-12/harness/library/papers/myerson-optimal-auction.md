# Incentive-compatible optimal auction design

## Problem

- maximize expected seller utility under private information
- characterize feasible allocation and payment rules

## Assumptions

- single indivisible object
- privately known bidder types with a specified prior environment
- Bayesian incentive and participation constraints

## Conclusion

The auction-design problem can be reduced to incentive-compatible direct mechanisms and solved through the paper's allocation and payment characterization.

## Method

mechanism-design optimization using incentive compatibility and expected revenue

## Proof strategy

- use direct revelation
- derive payment restrictions from incentive compatibility
- optimize the resulting virtual-surplus objective subject to monotonicity

## Failure conditions

- correlated values or interdependent values may require a different characterization
- irregular type distributions require care rather than naive pointwise allocation

## Scope note

This card records the canonical solution route, not an exhaustive statement of Myerson's assumptions.
