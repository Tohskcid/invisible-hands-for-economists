# Cyclic-monotonicity characterization of quasilinear implementability

## Problem

- test whether a multidimensional allocation rule is rationalizable
- construct transfers for quasilinear incentive compatibility

## Assumptions

- quasilinear preferences
- allocation or decision rule defined on a type domain
- the paper's regularity and domain conditions

## Conclusion

Rationalizability in the quasilinear setting is characterized by a cyclic condition stronger than one-dimensional monotonicity.

## Method

revealed-preference inequalities and convex analysis

## Proof strategy

- sum incentive inequalities around finite cycles
- construct a potential or transfer function when the cyclic condition holds

## Failure conditions

- ordinary pairwise monotonicity is generally insufficient in multiple dimensions
- non-quasilinear preferences fall outside the characterization

## Scope note

Inspect the exact domain and regularity conditions before claiming necessity or sufficiency.

## Primary source

A Necessary and Sufficient Condition for Rationalizability in a Quasi-Linear Context, Jean-Charles Rochet (1987), [Journal of Mathematical Economics](https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-4068(87)90007-3).
