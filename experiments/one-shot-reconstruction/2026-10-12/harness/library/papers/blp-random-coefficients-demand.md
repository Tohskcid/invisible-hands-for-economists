# Random-coefficients demand and supply estimation for differentiated products

## Problem

- estimate substitution patterns with aggregate product-level data
- address endogenous prices in differentiated-products demand
- recover demand and cost parameters consistent with oligopoly equilibrium

## Assumptions

- specified random-utility demand system
- instruments satisfying the model's orthogonality restrictions
- an oligopoly pricing model and conduct assumption for the supply side
- a valid market definition and observed product characteristics

## Conclusion

Aggregate shares can be inverted and combined with instruments and supply conditions to estimate heterogeneous demand and marginal-cost parameters.

## Method

share inversion, simulation, instrumental variables, and equilibrium first-order conditions

## Failure conditions

- weak or invalid instruments undermine price-effect identification
- conduct or market-definition misspecification distorts cost recovery
- numerical contraction or integration error can contaminate estimates

## Scope note

Treat demand, supply, instruments, and numerical tolerances as one joint research contract.
