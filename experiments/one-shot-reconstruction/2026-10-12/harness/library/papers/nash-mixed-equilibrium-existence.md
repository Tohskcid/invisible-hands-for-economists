# Mixed-strategy equilibrium existence in finite games

## Problem

- establish existence of a noncooperative equilibrium
- convert finite pure strategies to mixed strategies

## Assumptions

- finitely many players
- each player has finitely many pure strategies
- expected payoffs over mixed strategies

## Conclusion

A finite n-person game has an equilibrium in mixed strategies.

## Method

fixed-point existence argument on the product of mixed-strategy simplices

## Proof strategy

- define a best-response or countering correspondence
- apply Kakutani's fixed-point theorem

## Failure conditions

- infinite or noncompact strategy spaces require additional conditions
- discontinuous payoffs are outside the finite-game argument

## Scope note

Check the source and the hypotheses of the fixed-point theorem before adapting the result.
