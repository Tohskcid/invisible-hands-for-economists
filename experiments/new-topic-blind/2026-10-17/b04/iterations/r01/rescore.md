The new ironing argument appears internally coherent within r01's stated model. A nondecreasing own-value quality schedule gives an integration-by-parts upper bound using the convex minorant; fixed, report-independent ties make allocated quality constant on strict ironing intervals, so the constructed rule attains that bound. This assesses the proof's own claim, not the distinct continuum theorem in the target.

The score gain from **4/10 to 5/10** is attributable to the new irregular-score result and fully decreasing group-order implication. It is a reconstruction gain for this post-feedback revision. Historical novelty remains a separate question: `../../evaluation/unsealed/sealed/target.json` dates the earliest publicly verifiable target version to 2020-06-18, and the 2020 working paper already states its closure and across-group theorems (PDF pp. 14-16). This rescore makes no priority claim for r01's narrower finite-agent propositions.
