\section{Introduction}
We study the bargaining game [claim:thm-1] \cite{rubinstein1982}. The formal derivation is presented in Appendix A.
\section{Main Results}
\begin{theorem}
The unique subgame perfect equilibrium payoff to Player 1 is $x^* = \frac{1-\delta_2}{1-\delta_1\delta_2}$.
\end{theorem}
\section{Appendix: Mathematical Derivations}
Let $M_1$ and $m_1$ be the supremum and infimum of Player 1's SPE payoffs.
In round 2, Player 2 accepts any offer $y > \delta_2 M_1$, yielding $m_1 \ge 1 - \delta_2 M_2$.
Symmetrically, $M_1 \le 1 - \delta_2 m_2$. Solving yields $M_1 = m_1 = \frac{1-\delta_2}{1-\delta_1\delta_2}$.
