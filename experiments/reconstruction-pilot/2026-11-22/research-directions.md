# Research directions from quantitative syntheses

Curator notes, written separately from the author packets. Each question below is a proposal for a future experiment. None of the pooled results enter the two historical papers' scoring.

| Source and screening decision | Bounded question suggested by the source | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Doucouliagos and Stanley (2009), *British Journal of Industrial Relations*. A published meta-regression covering 64 US minimum-wage studies. [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-8543.2009.00723.x) | Among US employment studies with a comparable population and follow-up, how sensitive is the pooled estimate to the number of specifications contributed by each study? | Code the population, follow-up, employment unit, estimation design and estimate-to-study mapping. Compare a prespecified estimate per study with all eligible specifications using study-clustered uncertainty. Record overlap in the underlying policy episodes. |
| Dube and Zipperer (2024), NBER Working Paper 32925. A quantitative synthesis built around a common own-wage elasticity. The 2024 paper reports a set of 72 journal studies. The authors' evolving data release has its own inclusion rules and version. [Paper](https://www.nber.org/papers/w32925) · [Data documentation](https://economic.github.io/owe/documentation.html) | Within a fixed release, can differences in exposure to a wage floor explain heterogeneity in employment responses at a common horizon? | Match employment and wage effects for the same population, design and period. Record the sample restriction requiring a detectable positive wage response. Distinguish headcount, hours and establishments, and report the eligible subset for each comparison. |
| Smith and Huang (1995), *Journal of Political Economy*. A published meta-analysis of particulate air pollution and property values, based on hedonic models developed during 1967–1988. [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1086/261981) | Across the housing markets covered by the studies, how much does estimated willingness to pay for cleaner air vary after harmonizing pollution units and the dollar base year? | Link each estimate to its market, period, pollutant and units. Distinguish asset-price changes from annual flows. Assess dependence within studies and repeated use of the same market. |

The local 1990–2025 five-journal inventory contained 15,168 distinct DOIs. A case-insensitive title screen for meta-analysis, meta-analyses, meta-analytic or meta-regression, with an optional hyphen or space, returned two titles. Smith and Huang qualified. Anderson and Kichkha (2017) discuss research methods and provide no comparable collection of effects for these questions. The two labor sources above came from a separate targeted search. This screen misses syntheses with other wording and years missing from the provider. [Anderson and Kichkha](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.p20171033)
